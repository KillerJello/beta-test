How it works
  • Press and hold on your fist to stay in position. Strokes knock it out from under you — find it again fast.
  • The open hand — reaching back to cover — rides the same track, closer as it hurts more. Touch it, or drag through it, and your hand goes back.
  • Pain brings tears (the screen blurs, badly near your limit) and every stroke shakes you. The hardest go black for a moment.
  • Light implements come at an uneven pace and keep your grip busy, but you get more leeway. Heavy ones are steady and easy to hold between strokes — but the tears come fast.
  • Your real limit is hidden. Listen to the voice in your head. Orders and penalties always show clearly in the top bar.
  • Run out of poise and you get a break: the sting fades, the ache doesn't, and then it's back in position.
  • Keys that always work: Y yellow — slower and softer for a while · SPACE red — stop · E ask if that's enough.
